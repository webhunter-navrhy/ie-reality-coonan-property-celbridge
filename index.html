•Coonan Property present this superb A2-rated, four-bedroom detached residence, set within the exclusive and recently completed Williams Grove development

•Extending to approx. 2,003 sq.ft., this beautifully appointed home offers bright and generous accommodation comprising an impressive entrance hallway, elegant living room, separate TV/playroom, contemporary open-plan kitchen and dining area, utility room, four double bedrooms (two en suite), family bathroom and attic with folding ladder access

•Boasting an impressive A2 BER rating, complemented by an energy-efficient air-to-water heat pump heating system and high levels of insulation

•Thoughtfully designed with versatility in mind, offering adaptable living spaces suitable for a playroom, home office, gym or guest accommodation

•Presented in turnkey condition with exceptional finishes throughout, including a bespoke contemporary kitchen with feature island and quartz worktops, elegantly tiled bathrooms, wood-panelled walls, high-quality flooring and custom cabinetry

•Enjoys an enviable position overlooking a beautifully maintained green area, providing an attractive setting particularly suited to families with young children

•Within walking distance of the highly regarded St. Anne`s Primary School, local shop, GAA club, coffee shop, scenic Royal Canal walkway and Lyons Estate

•Excellent sporting and leisure amenities are within easy reach, including world-class golf at The K Club and Carton House, together with horse racing at Punchestown, Naas and The Curragh

•Ideally situated in the picturesque village of Ardclough, approximately 30 minutes from Dublin, with excellent connectivity via Hazelhatch Train Station (5km) and the nearby M7 (6km) and M4 (12km) motorways
